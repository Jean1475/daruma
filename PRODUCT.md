# Daruma

## Register
brand

## Product purpose
Daruma is a physical specialty store in Bilbao, Spain selling Pokémon TCG singles and sealed product, manga, and comics. The website is the store's public face: it showcases in-store stock, announces events (tournaments, prerelease, manga book club), explains the buy/trade service, and publishes a blog. There is no e-commerce — everything is sold in person only.

## Target users
- Pokémon TCG collectors and competitive players in and around Bilbao looking for specific cards
- Manga and comics readers who want a curated local alternative to online retail
- People with old collections looking to sell or trade
- Curious walk-ins discovering the store

## Brand personality
Editorial, physical, Japanese-influenced. The name "Daruma" references the Japanese wishing doll: you paint one eye when you start pursuing something, the second when you achieve it. This metaphor (wanting a card, finding it) runs through the copy. The aesthetic borrows from manga production: halftone textures, speed lines, kanji watermarks, stamp-style labels. The palette is a bold cobalt blue, warm gold, and cream — confident, not cute.

## Anti-references
- Generic e-commerce (Amazon, Cardmarket style grids)
- Pastel "collectibles" aesthetics (soft colors, rounded everything)
- Dark-mode "gaming" aesthetics (neon on black)
- SaaS landing pages with hero metrics and CTA stacks

## Design principles
1. Physical-first: the site should feel like a store, not an app. Tactile, printed-matter energy.
2. Curated: Daruma stocks what's worth stocking. The UI should be selective, not exhaustive.
3. Japanese + European: the dual identity (manga culture, Basque city) should be visible in type and texture choices.
4. No online sales: every CTA leads to visiting the physical store.

## Tech stack
Next.js 16 (App Router, Turbopack), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Supabase (auth + database), deployed on Vercel.
